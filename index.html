Our Mission

Community Dog Walks is a student-led volunteer initiative dedicated to helping neighbors care for their dogs. We believe everyone deserves support, especially when everyday tasks become difficult.

🐕 Completely Free

Our dog-walking service is provided at no cost to neighbors in need.

❤️ Community Focused

We're committed to helping people who could use extra assistance.
