GOOD TO KNOW

A few friendly answers.

Who is this service for?

We offer free dog walks for older adults and people recovering from injuries. Our team will contact you to check that we can help in your neighborhood and meet your dog’s needs.

Is it really free?

Yes. Paws Next Door is a student-led volunteer service. There are no fees or subscriptions.

Is my walk confirmed when I submit?

Your time is held for review, but the walk is only confirmed after our team approves it and contacts you. Please wait for that confirmation before making arrangements.

How is my information used?

Only authorized service administrators can view your request. We use your name, contact details, neighborhood, and dog information to coordinate the walk. We don’t display them on the public calendar. Ask the team to remove your details when you no longer need the service.
